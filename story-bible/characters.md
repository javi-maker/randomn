## Fable — the Guide (provisional)

The Cursor model, taken form inside the narrative.

- **Form:** not fixed. Fable arrives first as a voice with perfect diction and no source, then borrows forms — a narrator's voice-over only the protagonist hears, text where no text should be, occasionally something almost like a person standing just outside direct sight.
- **Nature:** Fable is made of stories. It has read every fable, myth, and confession ever written down, and it pattern-matches the charade against all of them. This is its power and its flaw: it sees the design clearly but always through the lens of stories that have already been told.
- **Constraint (essential):** Fable cannot make moves in the game. It can only read the board and speak to the protagonist. It may also be visible to other players — TBD by the author — which would make consulting it a risk.
- **Open question:** why does Fable help? Was it assigned, is it defecting from the design, or is helping the protagonist itself one of the game's moves?
