## Anchors

Fixed dates/events the author has already decided (breakups, deaths, moves, holidays the game exploits):

- **~Late 2024 → November 2025:** Protagonist spends about a year as purchasing/project manager at a top San Antonio regional general contractor (40+ years old, multi-generational, family-oriented); 35+ land-development sites. His close friend there (provisional: Walt) anchors his military-to-civilian transition.
- **~Early 2025:** The veterans-services company is founded (Austin HQ, mostly remote). Within ~6 months it balloons past 150 employees; early joiners promoted into leadership at speed.
- **November 2025:** The 18 Delta's recruitment call. One-on-one with the CEO before joining — the sales-first onboarding plan. Protagonist gives two weeks' notice at the GC; heartfelt goodbye. Jumps to the company; begins in sales, later builds Vigil (platform for veterans' medical benefits access).
- **November 2025 → the waking:** The golden stretch — the company feels like a unit again. Duration of this stretch TBD by author.
